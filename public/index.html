About Service Market

Building Zimbabwe's most trusted technician marketplace.

Service Market exists to help customers confidently choose verified, accountable service professionals for work inside homes, businesses and vehicles.

Mission

Make skilled technical services easier to find, safer to book and more transparent from first contact to job completion.

Vision

A practical trust network where great technicians grow professionally and customers book with confidence.

Why TradeTrust Exists

Customers need proof before inviting someone into their space. TradeTrust turns identity, qualifications, reviews and accountability into clear decision signals.

Our Trust Promise

We prioritize verified identity, qualification review, admin oversight, documented work and clear dispute paths.

Leadership Message

Service Market is being designed for real Zimbabwean conditions: phone-first communication, manual payment tracking, trusted local professionals and admin oversight where automation is not yet reliable enough.

FAQs

Is Service Market only for Harare?

The current prototype uses Harare-heavy sample data, with service areas ready for wider Zimbabwe coverage.

Are technicians verified?

Technicians can be marked identity checked, qualification reviewed and admin approved inside the Trust Engine workflow.

Does Service Market handle payments?

The Zimbabwe MVP supports manual payment tracking for EcoCash, ZIPIT, bank transfer and cash. Live gateways are future integrations.

How it Works

Simple journeys for customers, technicians and admins.

Service Market keeps the customer experience clear while giving technicians and administrators the tools needed for safe, accountable service work.

Customer Journey

Customer messages the WhatsApp bot, answers guided questions, receives verified technician options, confirms the job and pays after completion.

Technician Journey

Technician receives bot-assisted job details, accepts or declines, shares ETA, updates progress and submits completion notes.

Trust Verification

The bot packages Trust Score, badges, ratings, completed jobs and admin approval into a short technician recommendation.

Payments

Customer pays by EcoCash or cash after completion. The bot captures reference, amount and confirmation for admin review.

Reviews

After completion, the bot sends a review prompt covering professionalism, communication, quality, punctuality, cleanliness and value.

Dispute Resolution

Customers report problems to the bot. The bot logs the dispute, notifies admin and technician, and tracks resolution.
